# Ruffolo-Vitale Heritage Cookbook

A comprehensive digital preservation of the Ruffolo-Vitale family heirloom cookbook (329 pages), celebrating authentic Calabrian traditions, artisan pastas, Sunday gravies, holiday sweets, and time-tested kitchen wisdom.

## Features

- **Heirloom Recipe Collection**: 1,180+ family recipes from the original 329-page cookbook across 18 sections (Appetizers, Soups, Salads, Pasta & Sauces, Baked Pasta, Meats, Seafood, Breads & Pizza, Biscotti, Desserts, and Holiday Traditions).
- **Original Heritage Photography**: Includes original photographs connecting recipes to family traditions, rustic breadmaking, handmade gnocchi, and holiday scalille.
- **Smart Pantry Matcher ("What Can I Cook Tonight?")**: Enter on-hand ingredients to instantly cross-reference recipes, filter by 100% ready-to-cook or missing 1–2 items, with quick Italian pantry staple seeding.
- **Master A–Z Index**: Complete cross-indexed catalog browseable alphabetically, by cookbook chapter/section, or by family contributor (Nonna Rosina, Sandy, Ornella, Bruna, Elvira, etc.).
- **Dynamic Serving Scaler & Unit Converter**: Seamlessly scale ingredient quantities from 0.5x to 4x, with instant toggling between Imperial (cups, lbs, oz) and Metric (g, ml).
- **Interactive Kitchen Prep Checklist**: Check off ingredients as you prepare them at the counter.
- **Hands-Free Kitchen Cooking Mode**: Full-screen, step-by-step cooking interface designed for kitchen counters with high-legibility typography, step tracking, and quick ingredient drawer.
- **Global Multi-Timer Engine**: Background cooking timers that keep running while browsing, with audio-visual alerts and quick step timer presets.
- **Custom Notes & Spice Customization**: Adjust dish heat between Mild, Warm Kick, Spicy, and Calabrian Fire, and store personal cooking journal notes.
- **Print & Keepsake Book Export**: Print individual recipes or the complete heirloom volume to PDF or paper in traditional typography with family dedication foreword and roasting charts.
